Key Insights – Lenskart Analytics Dashboard.

1.	Sales are flat: ₹195–197M every year, growth within ±1.2%.
2.	February is the lowest month in all four years (seasonality; partly fewer days).
3.	Four product categories are balanced at ~25% each; the leader changes yearly.
4.	Under-25 customers contribute ₹113.8M (14.5%), about a third less than every other age group (~₹167M each).
5.	Five payment modes each take ~20% (₹154–158M).
6.	Online and Offline split ~50/50 in every category.

Limitations
	The dataset is synthetic (computer-generated), not real Lenskart transactions.
	Values are evenly distributed across categories, cities, genders and payment modes, so differences between segments are      small.
	The project demonstrates the end-to-end method (SQL → data model → DAX → dashboard), not real business findings.
